ABOUT US
The Vision Behind
Sweep Dreams

Born from a belief that every space deserves care and every client deserves complete trust. Sweep Dreams Building Solutions is a Black-owned, woman-led company in Dallas committed to raising the bar in an industry that too often settles for less.

Shardae Tillman
FOUNDER & CEO · SWEEP DREAMS BUILDING SOLUTIONS · DALLAS, TX

Shardae Tillman is the founder and CEO of Sweep Dreams Building Solutions, a Dallas based cleaning and facility services company she built from the ground up. A proud mother of three and Chicago native, she brings the same relentless work ethic and uncompromising attention to detail to every client she serves.

Before launching Sweep Dreams, Shardae spent more than 15 years in luxury retail and hospitality at some of the most prestigious brands in the world, including Louis Vuitton, Burberry, Neiman Marcus, and Nordstrom. There she mastered the art of white glove service, anticipating every client need and never settling for anything less than perfection. That standard became the foundation of everything Sweep Dreams stands for.

At Sweep Dreams, the luxury experience is not just a promise. It is personal.
